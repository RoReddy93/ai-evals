## Part 2 · Three Audience Messages

### A. For Engineering (Jira ticket)
Acceptance Criteria: GIVEN a user asks about pricing and the response includes a dollar amount, WHEN that amount differs from pricing_api.current_price, THEN block the response and display: “Live rates are unavailable. Please contact sales.”

Tolerance: No pricing discrepancies permitted.

Validation: Test 20 historical pricing queries. All quoted prices must match the API price.

### B. For UX / Design
When the pricing guardrail blocks a response, display: “Live pricing is unavailable. Please contact sales for the latest quote,” alongside a one-click “Contact Sales” button. Preserve the conversation context and never show a blank response or technical error.

### C. For Leadership (bi-weekly update)
We’ve implemented a pricing guardrail in Ascend IQ that blocks quotes that do not match the pricing API, addressing the revenue recognition risk identified in the M2 audit. Coverage: STRONG, enforced through a deterministic check. Estimated exposure mitigated: $X in enterprise contract liability, alongside reduced risk to customer trust. Performance is monitored weekly.
